# Visual QA

Review the page at a wide desktop viewport and at 360px before release.

Check:

- the program listing remains readable;
- the core panel keeps the instruction banner and metrics ordered;
- the register and RAM grids do not overflow;
- output values remain legible;
- hash values are truncated visually but available in the title attribute;
- controls remain reachable and focusable;
- no horizontal scrollbar appears on the mobile layout.

Screenshots are evidence of presentation only; they are not emulator or chain
proof.
